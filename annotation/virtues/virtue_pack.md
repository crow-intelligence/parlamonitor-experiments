# Hungarian virtue lexicon — for verification

Generated 2026-09-11 from `data/lexicons/virtues/hu_virtues.json` by `scripts/make_virtue_pack.py`.

## What is being measured

**virtue SALIENCE, not virtue possession.**

Which moral vocabulary a speaker reaches for. NOT whether they possess or cultivate the virtue, and NOT whether they are sincere. An MP who calls opponents liars is invoking truthfulness as a norm; an MP praising courage may be doing so ironically. Both count as engagement with the virtue, which is the construct.

**Not measured:** Character. Sincerity. Whether the speaker exhibits the virtue. A speaker may attribute a virtue to themselves, deny it of an opponent, or use it sarcastically, and the counts cannot tell these apart.

Each virtue has an `affirming` and an `accusing` list. Both are salience; the split records the stance. Reporting one number would hide it — 60% of truthfulness vocabulary in this corpus is accusation.

## How to verify

Fill the `verdict` column in `virtue_candidates.csv` with `accept`,
`reject`, or `move:<virtue>.<pole>`. Matching is on **lemmas**, so one
entry covers every inflection: `bátorság` catches *bátorságot,
bátorságunk, bátorsággal*.

## Words already excluded, and why

- **`igazság`** — means both truth and justice in Hungarian; assigning it to one is arbitrary, to both is double-counting
- **`igaz`** — also a discourse particle ('az igaz, hogy'); 295 hits would swamp truthfulness
- **`tisztelt`** — 3,592 hits, essentially all salutation ('Tisztelt Ház!')
- **`méltóság`** — 'emberi méltóság' is a constitutional term
- **`elismerés`** — wage policy — 'erkölcsi és anyagi elismerés'
- **`megbecsülés`** — wage policy — 'anyagi megbecsülés és a magasabb bér'
- **`hála`** — mostly the fixed expression 'hála istennek'
- **`nagyság`** — usually magnitude — 'a feladat nagysága'
- **`kiáll`** — also physically standing up
- **`fél`** — also 'half' and 'the other party'
